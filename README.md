# HR Attrition Analysis | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce data using Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into employee attrition, demographics, job satisfaction, performance, overtime, salary, and years at the company.

The goal of this project is to identify key factors associated with employee attrition and help HR teams make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments have the highest attrition rate?
- How does attrition vary by years at the company?
- Does overtime affect employee attrition?
- Which job roles have the highest attrition?
- How does attrition differ by gender and age?
- Is there a relationship between job satisfaction and attrition?
- How does attrition change over the years?
- How does salary level relate to employee attrition?
- What are the main workforce and performance trends?

## 🔍 Key Analysis

The dashboard focuses on understanding the factors that may contribute to employee attrition, including:

- Department
- Job Role
- Age
- Gender
- Salary
- Years at Company
- Job Satisfaction
- Overtime
- Performance Rating

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Overall employee attrition rate is approximately 16%.
- Attrition varies significantly across departments.
- Employees with fewer years at the company show higher attrition rates.
- Overtime is associated with a higher attrition rate.
- Attrition trends vary across different years and employee groups.
